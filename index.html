The way we work

A trading house built on two ports, one relationship at a time.

Veggie and Spicia Foodstuff Trading FZ‑LLC connects Indian growers, millers and processors directly to buyers in the UAE. We handle sourcing across multiple Indian states, quality checks before dispatch, and the shipping and customs process into the UAE — so wholesalers, restaurants and retailers get consistent stock without managing three vendors to do it.

The company is newly registered and building its first supply lines now. What stays constant: honest grading, realistic lead times, and someone who picks up the phone.
